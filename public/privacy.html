Privacy & private use

Dreams are intimate. This is how the prototype treats them. These terms are a working draft under legal review.

Where your dreams live

  • Your journal, your cast and your films are stored only in this browser (IndexedDB). We do not keep a copy on our servers.
  • Clearing your browser data deletes them. You can delete any dream or cast member in the app at any time.

What leaves your device

  • When you shape a dream, its text and your cast descriptions go through our server to OpenRouter and on to the AI model that writes the film script. We do not store them.
  • When you render, only the English film script goes to the video model. No names, no photos.
  • A photo you add to a cast member is sent once to write a text description and is then discarded. It is never saved and never sent to the video model.
  • Your OpenRouter key stays in your browser and is passed through our server only to reach OpenRouter. We do not log or store it.
  • If you join the waitlist we store your email address only, to tell you when early access opens.

Who pays

Everything runs on your own OpenRouter account. You pay the AI providers directly at their prices. We charge nothing in this prototype.

Private-use declaration

Before your first film you confirm that:

  • you are filming your own dream for private use,
  • you will not publish, distribute or earn money from the film,
  • you understand the film is AI-generated, and it is labelled as such,
  • people from your life appear only as text descriptions, never from photos of their faces.

Dreams that involve public figures are marked as private and can never be shared.

Safety

We do not film sexual content involving anyone who is or could appear to be under 18. If a dream suggests you may be thinking of hurting yourself, we will not film it and will point you to help instead. In Poland: 116 123 (adults), 116 111 (children and young people), emergency 112.
