How it works

Three steps from
dream to screen

01

Tell it half-awake

Write it the way you remember it, in any language. We ask at most one question, then shape it into four acts: threshold, encounter, ordeal, dissolution. You see every shot before anything is filmed.

02

Cast who keeps coming back

Your grandmother, the house by the lake, the stranger in the corridor. Describe them once — we keep them consistent across your dreams. A photo can help us write the description; we never store it.

03

Film it on your own account

Connect your OpenRouter account and render a 30-second film with Seedance. You pay the model directly, only for the films you choose to make. Every film is labelled as AI-generated and is for your private use.
