    Describe her once.
    Recognise her every time.

    Why four acts

    Your dream already has a structure

    Joseph Campbell found one arc beneath the world's myths. Carl Jung found a recurring cast beneath our dreams. Dreams rarely return or resolve, so the arc collapses into four moments, and four moments become four shots.

    I

    Threshold

    0–7s · wide

    There is no ordinary world in a dream. You arrive already inside the distortion: the familiar kitchen with the wrong ceiling.

    II

    Encounter

    7–15s · medium

    The figure arrives whether you're ready or not. The pursuer, the elder, someone you lost, wearing a familiar face.

    III

    Ordeal

    15–23s · close-up

    The image you woke up with. Rarely the most dramatic moment; more often a detail the camera refuses to leave.

    IV

    Dissolution

    23–30s · fade

    Dreams don't end, they stop. We end where you woke: in stillness, without forcing a resolution.
